# Link Genetic Patent Promise for the LinkID Specification

Version 1.0, effective 2026-10-08

## 1. Promise

Link Genetic GmbH, Zumikon, Switzerland, and its affiliates ("Link Genetic") irrevocably promise not to assert any Necessary Claims against any person or entity for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing, or distributing any implementation, to the extent that it conforms to a Covered Specification ("Covered Implementation").

This promise applies regardless of the development or distribution model of the Covered Implementation, including open source and commercial implementations. No fee or registration is required.

## 2. Covered Specifications

- draft-linkgenetic-linkid-uri-01, "The LinkID URI Scheme and Resolution Model"
  (https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-linkgenetic-linkid-uri/)

Link Genetic intends to add later revisions of this specification, including any RFC published from it, to this list. Versions added to this list remain covered permanently.

## 3. Necessary Claims

"Necessary Claims" are those claims of patents and patent applications, in any jurisdiction, owned or controlled by Link Genetic now or in the future, that are necessarily infringed by implementing the required portions of a Covered Specification. Required portions are those described in detail in the Covered Specification and not merely referenced by it.

Necessary Claims do not include claims that cover enabling technologies that may be necessary to make or use an implementation but are not themselves expressly set forth in a Covered Specification, or claims that cover features of an implementation that go beyond the required portions of a Covered Specification.

## 4. Defensive Suspension

This promise does not apply with respect to any person or entity that files, maintains, or voluntarily participates in a lawsuit or other proceeding asserting that an implementation of a Covered Specification by Link Genetic infringes a patent, from the date such proceeding is initiated.

## 5. Scope

This promise extends to developers, distributors, and users of Covered Implementations. It is not an assurance that any of Link Genetic's patent claims covers a Covered Implementation or is enforceable, or that a Covered Implementation does not infringe the rights of any third party.

This promise does not grant any rights in Link Genetic's trademarks or in any software not covered by its own license. Link Genetic's platform, services, and features beyond the Covered Specifications are not covered by this promise.
